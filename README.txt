MyNexus - Personal Hub & Life Organizer
========================================
Version 1.0.1  |  Developed by Sesank Koganti  |  2026

OVERVIEW
--------
MyNexus is a personal desktop organizer that helps you manage:

  * My Activities  - Track recurring bills, subscriptions, health visits,
                     and any task with automatic reminders
  * Connected Apps - Store all your app/website login details securely
                     in one place (bank, mortgage, insurance, utilities)
  * Document Vault - Keep important documents organized and searchable
                     (passports, tax returns, insurance policies, etc.)
  * Integrations   - Connect email providers for notification delivery
  * Dashboard      - See what's due this week and what's overdue at a glance
  * Settings       - Configure reminders, notifications, and backups

GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Launch MyNexus from the Start Menu or Desktop shortcut
2. On first launch, sample data is pre-loaded so you can explore every feature
3. Update the placeholder data with your real information
4. Enable notifications in Settings > Notifications

YOUR DATA
---------
All personal data is stored ONLY on your computer at:
  C:\Users\<YourName>\AppData\Roaming\MyNexus\data\

Data is NEVER sent to any server. Updates and reinstalls do NOT delete your data.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
-------------------
  * Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  * 100 MB free disk space
  * No internet connection required (offline-first)

SUPPORT

UNINSTALLING
------------
Go to Windows Settings > Apps > MyNexus > Uninstall
Your personal data in AppData will be preserved.
