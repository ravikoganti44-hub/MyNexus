# Document Vault - Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Access Document Vault
1. Open MyNexus application
2. Click **📁 Document Vault** in the sidebar

### Step 2: Upload Your First Document
1. Click **⬆ Upload Document** button
2. Select a file (PDF, image, Word doc, etc.)
3. Enter a title (e.g., "My Passport")
4. Choose a category (Passports, Tax Documents, etc.)
5. Click **Upload**

### Step 3: View Your Documents
1. Browse documents by clicking categories on the left
2. Click **👁 Preview** to open any document
3. Click **☆** to mark favorites (becomes **★**)

### Step 4: Monitor Expiry Dates
1. Go to the **Statistics** tab
2. Set expiry dates when uploading documents
3. Get alerts for documents expiring soon

## Common Tasks

### Organize Tax Documents by Year
1. Category: **Tax Documents**
2. Sub-Category: **2024** (or relevant year)
3. Upload your tax return PDF

### Store Passport Information
1. Category: **Passports**
2. Reference Number: Enter your passport number
3. Set expiry date for your passport
4. Upload passport scan

### Find a Document Quickly
1. Use the **search box** at the top
2. Type document name or partial information
3. Results appear instantly

### Access Important Documents
1. Go to **Favorites** tab
2. All your starred documents appear here
3. Quick access to frequently needed files

## Tips & Tricks

✨ **Pro Tips:**

1. **Use consistent naming** - "Tax Return 2024" instead of "tax2024finalversion"
2. **Set expiry dates** - Keep track of document validity
3. **Add descriptions** - Note important details in the description field
4. **Use reference numbers** - Store passport/license numbers for easy identification
5. **Organize by year** - For recurring documents, use sub-categories for years
6. **Star favorites** - Quick access to frequently needed documents
7. **Search effectively** - Use keywords from title, description, or tags

## FAQ

**Q: Can I upload any file type?**  
A: Yes, the vault accepts PDF, images (JPG, PNG), Word documents, Excel files, text files, and more.

**Q: Where are my documents stored?**  
A: Documents are stored locally on your computer in the `data/documents/` folder.

**Q: Can I access my documents from another computer?**  
A: Yes, by backing up and transferring your `data/documents/` folder and database file.

**Q: Will I be notified about expiring documents?**  
A: Check the Statistics tab for documents expiring soon. Future versions will include notifications.

**Q: Can I delete a document?**  
A: Yes, you can delete documents, or archive them to hide without deletion.

**Q: Is my data encrypted?**  
A: Currently, documents are stored locally. Future versions will include encryption options.

**Q: How much can I store?**  
A: Limited only by your computer's storage space.

**Q: Can I search all my documents?**  
A: Yes, use the search box to find documents across all categories.
