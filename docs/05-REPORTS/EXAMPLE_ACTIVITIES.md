## How to Use These Examples

1. Open ProJ Connect
2. Go to "My Activities"
3. Click "+ Add Activity"
4. Copy information from examples above
5. Adjust dates to match your schedule
6. Save and receive reminders!

## Tips for Success

✅ **Be Specific** - Use exact names (not "Car stuff")
✅ **Add Details** - Include account numbers, phone numbers in notes
✅ **Set Realistic Reminders** - 1-2 days for payments, immediately for tickets
✅ **Use Categories** - Makes filtering and organization easier
✅ **Update Status** - Mark complete after finishing activity
✅ **Track History** - Check completion history for patterns

## Customization Ideas

- Add specific amounts due (in notes field)
- Include contact information
- Add confirmation numbers
- Track service provider names
- Note payment methods used
